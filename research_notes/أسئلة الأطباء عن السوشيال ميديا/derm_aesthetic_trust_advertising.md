## Q1. What do aesthetic/derm doctors ask about before/after photos, consent, what's allowed, filters, being reported/fined?

### Takeaway
Doctor-side evidence I could source is regulator/press, not doctors' own posts. The rule signals: written patient consent is the baseline in Egypt (draft), Saudi (fines applied), and Dubai goes further (surgical filming for promotion banned even with consent). No doctor first-person question about this was retrievable.

## Q4. Questions repeated across independent sources / ranked items (max 8 allowed; only 3 clear the bar, all weak)

Bar: has a source URL + quote in a real person's/publication's words. None is a confirmed Arabic audience question. Counts = independent sources I saw (not verified by opening).

### Item 1 - "ازاي أعرف إن صور قبل وبعد حقيقية مش متعدّلة؟" (How do I know the after photo isn't edited?)
- Type: objection (patient). Platform: Reddit (relayed), explainer articles. Date: 2025-02-12 (PetaPixel).
- Quote [relayed]: Reddit users called the surgeon's images "an organized scam" - [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/02/12/plastic-surgeon-accused-of-photoshop-editing-viral-before-and-after-images-of-clients). The Arabic wording above is MY translation, not a quote.
- Independent sources: ~4 (PetaPixel, RealSelf, Save Face, Nuffield, Psychology Today stat; Erem News for an Arabic-world case). Strongest repeated topic found.
- Video idea: "افحص صورة قبل/بعد في 20 ثانية": same light, same angle, same distance, no makeup, mid-term photo. Doctor shows own pair side-by-side with phone-camera raw metadata/same setup.
- Hooks: "صورة بعد أحلى من الواقع؟ دي 3 حاجات تكشفها." / "قبل ما تصدق أي قبل وبعد، بصّ على الإضاءة."
- Format: talking head + screen split of two photos with lighting trick demo. CTA: "ابعت لي صورتين وأقولك لو فيهم حاجة مش مظبوطة" (comment keyword). Difficulty: easy.

### Item 2 - "ممكن أنشر صور مرضاي قبل وبعد؟ وإيه اللي ممنوع؟" (What can I post without risking the syndicate?)
- Type: question (doctor) - INFERRED from rule coverage; no doctor quote found. Include only as topic anchored to sources.
- Sources: Akhbar El Yom draft appendix (written consent, no children) [link above]; Elbalad (4 doctors investigated, deletion not enough) [link above]; Masrawy (syndicate vs unlicensed centres, 2025-10-04).
- Independent sources: 3 press items, same regulator, so 1 voice really. Egypt only.
- Idea: "3 حاجات الطبيب لازم يخليها مكتوبة قبل أي كاميرا": written consent specific to social media, face/identifying features, right to withdraw. Must say it is draft/under discussion in Egypt, not final law. Verify with syndicate before publishing.
- Hooks: "نقابة الأطباء بتحقق في فيديوهات دكاترة تجميل، الحذف مش كفاية." / "موافقة المريض الشفوية مش تحمي حضرتك."
- Format: face to camera, text cards of consent clauses. CTA: "احفظ الفيديو وابعته لزميل." Difficulty: medium (needs rule verification).

### Item 3 - Gulf: "فيه حاجة اسمها تصوير العمليات للدعاية؟" (Can I film procedures for promotion?)
- Type: question (doctor) - inferred; rule-confirmed only. Dubai DHA bans surgical filming for ads even with consent (2018, Emarat Al Youm + The National); Saudi MOH fined 10k SAR for photo without consent (2014).
- Independent sources: 3 outlets for 2 regulators. Dated - warn.
- Idea: "في دبي حتى لو المريض وافق، ممنوع": country-by-country comparison reel. Hooks: "المريض وقّع وبرضه ممنوع؟ في دبي أيوه." / "غرامة 10 آلاف ريال على صورة واحدة؟" Format: map/flag cards. CTA: "اسأل جهتك قبل ما تصوّر." Difficulty: medium (needs current-rule check).
