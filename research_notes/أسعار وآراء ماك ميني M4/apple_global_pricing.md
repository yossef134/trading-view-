## 1. Launch prices, configurations and upgrade prices (Oct 2024)

### Takeaway
M4 Mac mini launched at $599 (16GB/256GB) and M4 Pro at $1,399 (24GB/512GB, 12-core CPU/16-core GPU); a 14-core/20-core GPU M4 Pro was +$200. RAM and SSD upgrades followed the usual Apple $200/$400 ladder, and neither can be added after purchase.

### Cited Findings
- Base M4 (10-core CPU/10-core GPU, 16GB, 256GB SSD) launched at $599; M4 Pro (12-core CPU/16-core GPU, 24GB, 512GB) at $1,399; the full 14-core CPU/20-core GPU M4 Pro cost $200 more — [Macworld M4 Pro Mac mini review](https://www.macworld.com/article/2512911/m4-pro-mac-mini-review-remarkably-small-and-incredibly-powerful.html), [TechRepublic launch coverage](https://www.techrepublic.com/de/article/apple-mac-mini-m4-launch/)
- US launch upgrade prices (base M4): 16GB to 24GB = +$200; to 32GB = +$400; 256GB to 512GB = +$200; to 1TB = +$400. A 2TB step was not confirmed in sources (pattern would suggest +$800 but this is unverified) — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2512911/m4-pro-mac-mini-review-remarkably-small-and-incredibly-powerful.html)
- Launch education pricing (US): M4 from $499; M4 Pro from $1,299 — search summary of launch coverage; see [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2512911/m4-pro-mac-mini-review-remarkably-small-and-incredibly-powerful.html) and [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/de/article/apple-mac-mini-m4-launch/)
- The 16GB/512GB M4 config was $799 at launch (it was already $799 before the 2026 changes) — [MacRumors, 1 May 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/01/mac-mini-now-starts-at-799/)
- M4 Pro launch price was $100 above the prior M2 Pro mini ($1,299); UK M4 Pro price quoted as £1,399 — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2512911/m4-pro-mac-mini-review-remarkably-small-and-incredibly-powerful.html)
- Netherlands launch-era upgrade examples: 24GB = +€230; 2TB storage = +€920 — [iCulture](https://www.iculture.nl/nieuws/mac-mini-m4-prijzen/)
- Brief spec context (from my background knowledge, not re-verified this session): M4 has 2 front USB-C, 3 rear Thunderbolt 4, HDMI, Gigabit Ethernet (10GbE optional); M4 Pro has 3 Thunderbolt 5 rear. Mini is about 5 x 5 in. Treat as unsourced.

### Inferences
- The tier ladder is: M4 16/256 ($599), 16/512 ($799), 24/512, 32GB options, M4 Pro 24/512 ($1,399), M4 Pro 14-core ($1,599 at launch), with Pro RAM up to 64GB.

### Gaps
- Full launch config list (every RAM/SSD/10GbE combo) and 2TB/8TB prices were not retrievable; Apple's pages could not be fetched.
- Launch prices for UK, EU, UAE, KSA, Egypt not retrieved (only the £1,399 M4 Pro figure above).

## 2. Current official price (Oct 2026): M4 has been repriced and the line has a successor

### Takeaway
The $599 256GB M4 was pulled around 1 May 2026 (entry became $799), the M4 Pro start price rose $200 to $1,599 on 25 June 2026, and Apple then announced a successor lineup on 25 Aug 2026 (Mac mini with M6 and M5 Pro, on sale 22 Sept 2026, from $899 and $1,699). I did not find a source showing whether Apple still lists any M4 mini on its own store in Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Around 1 May 2026 Apple quietly removed the 256GB M4 mini (US $599); the entry model became M4/16GB/512GB at $799. The $799 512GB config itself was not repriced. M4 Pro starting price was unchanged at that time — [MacRumors, 1 May 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/01/mac-mini-now-starts-at-799/), [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/3129806/mac-mini-starting-price-rises-to-799-as-apple-stops-offering-256gb-option.html), [MacDailyNews](https://macdailynews.com/2026/05/01/apple-discontinues-599-base-mac-mini-entry-level-model-now-starts-at-799-with-512gb-storage-amid-surging-demand/)
- Reason: AI-driven demand and memory/chip supply tightness; Tim Cook cited higher-than-expected demand for the mini for AI/agentic tools on the fiscal Q2 2026 call. Higher-memory configs showed backorders; some 10-12 week delivery times — [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2164036/why-apple-discontinued-599-base-model-mac-mini/), [RedShark News](https://www.redsharknews.com/apple-mac-mini-price-rises-599-discontinued), [Fortune, 2 May 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/05/02/apple-mac-minis-starting-price-hike-799-ai-demand-supply-shortage/)
- The discontinuation of the 256GB model was described as worldwide — [The Tech Outlook](https://www.thetechoutlook.com/new-release/gadgets-release/apple-discontinues-the-mac-mini-m4-256gb-model-worldwide/), [Notebookcheck](https://notebookcheck.net/Official-Apple-price-hikes-kick-off-with-Mac-Mini-is-MacBook-Neo-next-or-can-Tim-Cook-avoid-a-Windows-level-crisis.1288006.0.html)
- 25 June 2026: Apple raised prices across all current Macs, iPads, Apple TV, HomePod, Vision Pro (iPhone, Watch, accessories, displays spared). Apple statement: "We have never seen a component price increase this much, this quickly." Reported Mac rise about 15-20%. Examples: MacBook Neo $599 to $699, MacBook Air 512GB $1,099 to $1,299, MacBook Pro 1TB $1,699 to $1,999 — [EveryMac](https://everymac.com/systems/by_shipping/june-2026-apple-price-hike.html), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/06/25/apple-price-increases-mac-ipad-more/), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-apple-price-hikes-june-2026/)
- M4 Pro Mac mini starting price rose $200 from $1,399 to $1,599 on 25 June 2026 — [MacRumors forums](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/apple-hikes-m4-pro-mac-mini-starting-price-amid-rising-memory-costs.2484529/), [MacObserver](https://www.macobserver.com/news/apple-raises-mac-mini-m4-pro-price-by-200-amid-rising-component-costs/) (headline only). Only forum/secondary-source confirmation; I did not read Apple's store. A MacRumors forum commenter claimed a 16/256 config returned at $799 — unconfirmed.
- Successor: Apple announced the Mac mini with M6 and M5 Pro on 25 Aug 2026; available 22 Sept 2026. US start prices: M6 (16GB/256GB) $899 ($799 education); M5 Pro $1,699 ($1,599 education). M5 Pro supports up to 64GB; Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 — [Apple Newsroom, Sept 2026](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/the-new-mac-mini-and-mac-studio-are-available-today/), [MacRumors, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/25/apple-announces-2026-mac-mini/), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/25/apple-announces-new-mac-mini-heres-everything-new/), [iClarified](https://www.iclarified.com/p1301/mac-mini-m6-16gb-ram-256gb-ssd)

### Inferences
- Apple's own M4 pricing in Oct 2026 is likely the post-hike level ($799 for 16/512; M4 Pro from $1,599), meaning M4 is now more expensive than at launch, not cheaper. Because the successor base ($899) is only $100 above the $799 M4, M4 has little price room to fall on Apple's own store; discounts occur at third-party retailers.
- Apple likely stopped selling M4 minis on its own store after 22 Sept 2026 (typical practice), but this is unverified.
