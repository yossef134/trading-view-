## 4. Is Egypt much higher than US/Gulf, and why?

### Takeaway
Yes. Egyptian new prices are about 1.5-1.9x the US list price and about 1.4-1.6x Gulf shelf prices for comparable configs. The sources I found do not break down the causes for Macs specifically; the explanations below are partly inference.

### Cited Findings
- US list prices: M4 Mac mini started at $599 (16GB/256GB) until May 2026; since then base is $799 for 16GB/512GB. M4 Pro 24GB/512GB launched at $1,399 (Amazon/B&H sold it at $1,299 in Oct 2025 and Macworld reported $1,247). — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/01/apple-discontinues-base-mac-mini-now-starts-at-799-with-512gb-storage/); [AppleInsider Oct 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/10/17/price-war-apples-m4-pro-mac-mini-drops-to-1299)
- US street deals on the old 256GB model reached $499; 24GB/512GB around $849 in mid-2025. — [AppleInsider Jun 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/06/16/flash-deal-m4-mac-mini-drops-to-499-in-todays-price-war)
- Egypt customs context: logistics vendors cite 5-20% customs duty on IT equipment plus 14% VAT (not an official tariff schedule; approximate). For phones, Egypt applies a combined 37.5% in charges and in Jan 2026 ended the passenger customs exemption for imported phones. I found nothing equivalent specific to desktop Macs. — [Growrk Egypt guide](https://growrk.com/country-guide-egypt); [Egyptian Streets, 20 Jan 2026](https://egyptianstreets.com/2026/01/20/egypt-ends-customs-exemption-on-imported-phones-carried-by-passengers/); [MEA Tech Watch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/03/25/ntra-reminds-egyptian-customers-of-customs-duty-payment-deadline-for-imported-mobile-devices/)
- Exchange rate context: about 49.8-51 EGP/USD in Jul-Aug 2026 (52-week range roughly 46.6-54.8). — [Goldpricez](https://goldpricez.com/currency-rates/egypt); [Pluang](https://pluang.com/en/tools/currency-converter/usd-egp); [Arab Finance, 27 Jul](https://arabfinance.com/en/news/newdetails/usd-egp-exchange-rates-down-on-july-27th)

### Inferences (my arithmetic at ~50 EGP/USD)
| Config | Egypt (EGP) | ~USD | US list | Egypt/US ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M4 16/256 (Amazon.eg, ~47-50k) | 47,000-50,000 | 940-1,000 | $599 (street $499) | about 1.6x list |
| M4 16/512 (Amazon.eg, 59k) | 59,000 | 1,180 | $799 | about 1.5x |
| M4 24/512 (2B, 90,999) | 90,999 | 1,820 | about $999 launch list (my knowledge; 2025 street about $849) | about 1.8x |
| M4 Pro 24/512 (Tradeline 109,900 to Amazon.eg 145,000) | 109,900-145,000 | 2,200-2,900 | $1,399 | about 1.6-2.1x |
| Gulf comparison: UAE 16/256 AED 2,499 | n/a | 680 | $599 | about 1.14x of US (incl. 5% VAT) |

- Drivers (inference, not confirmed by a Mac-specific source): customs duty plus 14% VAT; importers pricing in a weak/volatile pound and adding risk margin; no Apple Store in Egypt, so supply is via distributors/resellers with their own margins; retail-side installment financing costs; and global memory-driven supply constraints in 2026.
- A buyer in Egypt could plausibly save by buying in the UAE (about $680 for a 256GB unit) or Japan, but this brings warranty and duty questions (section 5); no source confirms what duty a traveller pays on a Mac mini carried into Egypt.

### Gaps
- No Egypt-specific source on Mac/desktop customs rates or on how retailers set prices.
- No official Apple Egypt price list found (apple.com blocked; Apple has no online store in Egypt per my knowledge, not verified in this session).

## 5. Warranty: authorised vs imported / gray-market units in Egypt

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. Apple says it may restrict service for some products to the country of original sale; a 2023 article names Tradeline and Switch Plus as authorised Apple resellers in Egypt. I found no Mac-mini-specific, Egypt-specific warranty policy for imported units.

### Cited Findings
- Apple support text (via forum): Apple may restrict warranty service for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and HomePod to the country where Apple or its Authorized Distributors originally sold the device. Mac is not named in that list, which suggests Mac desktops are less restricted, but I did not confirm this on Apple's own page. — [Apple Support Communities](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/250922382)
- A forum thread on a UAE-bought Mac states the one-year limited warranty runs from original retail purchase date and a purchase receipt is needed for service. — [Apple Support Communities](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/250922382)
- Enterprise (2023) says Egypt has two authorised Apple resellers, Tradeline and Switch Plus, and that authorised resellers in Egypt cannot repair iPad screens or Apple Watches. Dated. — [Enterprise Egypt](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2023/04/20/so-you-broke-your-iphone-and-cant-replace-it-now-what/)
- Gulf "Middle East Version" units (e.g. SharafDG KSA listing) carry the regional SKU designation; no source states how Apple Egypt treats them. — [Pricena KSA](https://sa.pricena.com/en/product/apple-mac-mini-2024-m4-with-10-core-cpu-16gb-ram-256gb-ssd-10-core-gpu-macos-sequoia-silver-middle-east-version-price-in-saudi-arabia-165171273)

### Inferences
- Buying from Tradeline (authorised, price 109,900 for M4 Pro) vs a marketplace seller on Amazon.eg/Dubizzle means paying a premium for a local invoice/warranty; for imported units, the safest approach is to ask the seller for the serial number and check coverage at checkcoverage.apple.com (my suggestion, not from a source), and to keep the original receipt.
- Used Dubizzle units under their original (possibly foreign) warranty may or may not be serviceable in Egypt; unverified.

### Gaps
- No official Apple Egypt warranty page for imported Macs; no Egypt customs rule for Macs; no consumer reports of repair experiences with Gulf-purchased Mac minis in Egypt; unconfirmed which Egyptian stores are currently authorised (2B, B.TECH, Raya status unknown).
