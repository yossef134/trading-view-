# Content formats 2026 (reference vocabulary)

Source: the user's course handout "أقوى فورمات المحتوى في 2026" (Chapter 2, lesson 10, updated July 2026).
Its rule: **niche first** - pick the formats that fit the specialty, not the ones that merely go viral.

**This is a vocabulary, not a closed list.** When a reel does not fit, give it a new descriptive name
(e.g. `whiteboard explainer + face PiP`) and keep it. Do not force reels into these ten.

| # | Format | What it is | Use it when | Execution notes from the handout |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split Screen** | Top: something that grabs the eye (AI clip, screen recording, a demo, existing footage). Bottom: you talking. | Success is not one-off; comparing options; fitness/commitment; any idea that needs a visual | Easiest: grab ready footage and talk over it. Strongest: make the top yourself (AI, real shots, montage). **Warning:** a fun top clip unrelated to your message wins the view, not the value |
| 2 | **Green Screen** | You in front of another video / screenshot / post / news / client result / something you made | Short idea, not long | Speak from YOUR angle even over someone else's content; a funny clip alone gets a laugh and no follow |
| 3 | **Raw Talking** | You talk to camera in an ordinary place (couch, kitchen counter, desk, walking) | Personal stories, lessons from mistakes, hard decisions, experience-based advice | Looks casual, but the idea must be clear and **scripted**; trust builds with repetition |
| 4 | **Busy Videos** | Raw talking while you do something (tidy the desk, pack a bag, cook, train) | Same as Raw Talking | The action can match the talk (planning your day at the calendar) or not (coffee while talking about parenting); test both |
| 5 | **Reaction** | You react to someone's clip | You add value: **agree** (this is real, I tried it), **clarify** (simplify a sentence from your field), **respectful disagreement** (I see another angle) | **Only exception for criticism:** someone in your field gives dangerous misinformation - hide their name/face, attack the idea not the person |
| 6 | **Podcast Style** | Mic in front, eye-level camera, as if talking to a guest | Explaining an idea, market opinion, personal experience, answering an objection, analysing a position | Camera at eye level; never look down at the table |
| 7 | **Clone Videos** | Two versions of you on one screen: the right one and the wrong one | Right vs wrong thinking, aware vs unaware client, old vs new version, cheap vs expensive selling | Locked camera, wide frame, space between the two, fixed light. Search YouTube "How to clone yourself" |
| 8 | **Overlay Text** | Written text over relevant footage | You have no time to shoot; short advice, lesson learned, summary story, motivation, before/after | 5-10 sentences; footage must relate to the message; prefer your own footage; a personal brand still needs people to see and hear you |
| 9 | **Best Of** | Footage + a title, you react to a list | You want reach without being very visible: best 5 channels / podcasts / books / tools in your field | Count down from last to first (10...1): curiosity keeps people to the end, retention feeds the algorithm |
| 10 | **Questions** | Someone off-camera asks you | S-tier ranking (rank 6 things in your field S/A/B...), True/False (put a claim in the right box and say why) | What matters is your opinion, not the ranking itself |

## Formats seen in practice that are NOT in the ten

From the first real run (5 Arabic doctor-marketing accounts, 10 winning reels viewed on 2026-11-04):

- **whiteboard explainer + face PiP** - handwritten iPad board, small face thumbnail, title card in the first 2.5 s, 130-180 s. The biggest winners in that sample (45K-68K views for a 5.6K-follower account).
- **screen-share walkthrough + face** (a Split Screen variant) - a spreadsheet on top, the speaker below.
- **talking head + motion-graphic cutaways** - high production: styled set, kinetic text, illustrations, glass-UI overlays.
- **podcast clip + text-card hook** - a black card with the hook text in the first 0.2-2.5 s, then a studio-sofa clip.
- **talking head + word-by-word captions (+ screen insert / stickers / numbered cards)**.

Treat these as examples of how to name what you see, not as a second fixed list.

## How the skill uses this file

1. Label every competitor reel whose frames were viewed with a format (free-form, using the names above where they fit).
2. Give every video idea 1-2 formats, with a production difficulty and whether the client must appear on camera.
3. Match formats to the client's constraints from the niche interview (shows face? time per week?).
