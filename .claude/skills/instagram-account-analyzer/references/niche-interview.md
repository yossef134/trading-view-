# Niche interview

Goal: write the niche as ONE sentence the client confirms:

> I help **[exactly who]** get **[exactly what outcome]** through **[how / what offer]**.

Never start scraping competitors, hashtags or research until this sentence exists. If the account already
shows it clearly (bio + captions agree), propose a draft and ask only to confirm or correct it.
If it does not, ask - do not guess.

## How to ask

- 3-4 questions per round, multiple choice where possible (AskUserQuestion), one free-text option always.
- After each round restate what you understood in one line and say what is still unclear.
- Stop when the sentence is specific enough that two competitors could be named from it, and the client says yes.

## Question bank (pick what is missing)

**Who**
- Which exact profession / segment? (e.g. "dentists", not "doctors"; "owners of private clinics", not "medical")
- Seniority or size (new graduate, clinic owner, hospital employee)? Country and dialect of the audience?
- What do they currently struggle with, in their own words?

**Outcome and offer**
- What result do you promise and in what time frame? Is there proof (numbers, clients, before/after)?
- What do you sell (service, course, consultation) and at what price band?
- What is the call to action you want after a video (DM, WhatsApp, link, follow)?

**Where the client is**
- Zero followers, or an audience and content already working? How long has it been running?
- What has worked or failed so far, in their own view?

**Constraints (they decide which formats are usable)**
- Will you appear on camera? Voice only? Time per week? Who edits?
- Language and dialect? Topics you will not touch?
- Competitors or accounts you admire, and accounts you refuse to look like.

**Goal**
- What counts as success in 90 days: followers, booked calls, sales, authority?

## Stage (decide with the client)

| Stage | Public signal | What changes in the analysis |
|---|---|---|
| `zero` | under 1,000 followers or under 15 posts | Skip auditing own posts; spend the effort on niche, competitors, formats and the first 12 videos |
| `early` | 1K-10K or irregular posting | Audit own content, weigh competitor patterns heavily |
| `working` | 10K+ with regular posting | Deep own-content audit, then compare to competitors |

`analysis.json -> stage` proposes one from public numbers; always confirm it.

## Output of the interview

Save in the report: niche sentence, stage, constraints (on camera / time / editing / dialect), goal,
and the 5 sub-niches the client approved.
