# STORYLINES: الإعلان الممول ضد المحتوى

Format: Reel 9:16, 1080×1920, 30fps. Length 60–75 s (the approved script is ~170 words).
Source: Yossef's approved script (2026-10-28). All on-screen copy is taken verbatim from it.

## B (recommended): الرسايل اللي بتوصلك
- Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
- What the viewer sees: two phones side by side. Left: an ad that keeps pushing, and DMs that all say «بكام؟» «فيه خصم؟». Right: posts showing up again and again, and one DM that says «عايز أحجز». Then the screen collapses into the right phone.
- First 2 s: the line «مش هتعرف تسوّق لعيادتك صح... غير لما تعمل إعلان ممول» stamped huge, and «يا ترى الكلام ده صح؟» lands under it.
- Beats: hook → left phone: reach climbs and haggling DMs pile up → rehook «طب إيه فايدة المحتوى الأورجانيك؟» → right phone: same face every day, "this is talking to me", booking → payoff: the ad as a magnifier over both → «استمراريتك... أهم مليون مرة» → follow + name.
- Look: warm off-white ground, deep navy ink, one coral accent for «صح؟» and «مليون مرة», Arabic display face (Alexandria/Cairo 800).
- Sound: soft bed, a notification ping on each DM, a dull thud on the haggling, a clean chime on «عايز أحجز».

## A: الكلام هو البطل
- Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
- Giant Arabic kinetic type on full-colour fields that switch every half beat. No UI, no phones: the words alone.
- First 2 s: «إعلان ممول» fills the frame, then a big «؟» hits.
- Beats: hook → «مليون إعلان» → «فصال وخلاص» → rehook → «بيشوفك باستمرار» → «بيكلمه هو بس» → «مليون مرة» → name.
- Look: ink-black and fire-orange planes, bold condensed Arabic (Cairo 900), hard cuts.
- Sound: a punchy rhythmic bed, a cut on every hit.

## C (bold): مكبّر الصوت
- Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
- One megaphone (the ad) in the middle of the frame. Whatever you put behind it gets louder: valuable content → more trust; discounts → more haggling. The frame keeps rearranging around it.
- First 2 s: a megaphone blasting the word «إعلان» at the camera, and the question lands.
- Beats: hook → megaphone amplifies empty noise → haggling grows → rehook → content cards behind the megaphone → amplified into trust → «الأساس» → «مليون مرة» → name.
- Look: flat paper cut-out shapes, cream + cobalt + a red accent, a single stage.
- Sound: amplified hum, a muffled noise for the haggling, a clean tone for trust.
